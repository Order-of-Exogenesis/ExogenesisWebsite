About the project

Prototyping a scientifically accurate evolution game

Exogenesis is a project found by Quintessence, where the player takes a role of a small life organism, and guides its species through billions of years of evolution, changing and transforming, until reaching sapience, building society, and finally, grasping cosmos. Decisions, changes and fate of the species are decided only by the player. And with this freedom, comes the responsibility - the final result is also only up to you.

Exogenesis is yet in an active prototype development state, but already has playable prototypes of several stages and lots of plans on development in near future.

Scientific accuracy, but with an interesting approach

Exogenesis tries to keep things relatively simple and very interesting, while maintaining scientifically accurate educational gameplay.

Procedural generation

Randomised worlds, lots of opportunities for role-playing, infinite configurations. The idea of the game is to offer as much as possible to anyone who loves not only playing, but diving into it.

Stable updates

Maybe not really fast, but the game is updated regularly, Quintessence will always work on this project till he dies.

Community ideas

Ambitious and unique ideas are always welcomed, Quintessence really likes the power of community.
Anyone can become a developer; the help is always welcomed!

Always original

Whatever form the game has, no matter how it looks, it will always remain original first. Innovative ideas and new approaches to Spore-likes is one of the core principles.

No AI. No money spent.

The budget of the project is Quintessence's personal insanity, and the help of anyone who will offer their support! And even in such state, no AI-generated assets will ever be used in the project.

STAGES

Choose an origin of life

Life can begin in many ways; like Exogenesis - where the life is brought by a cosmic meteor - or Abiogenesis, where the life on your planet slowly evolved from simple chemical compounds.

Survive as a cell

Collect nutrients, complete tasks, avoiding dangerous environments and migrating into different regions. Evolve to adapt to your surroundings better, and progress towards becoming multicellular life.

Fight for your life

Interact with other cells - randomly generated or pre-made organisms populating your planet. Some of them are peaceful and passive, some are just seeking for nutrients, and some will attack and eat you.

Explore the underwater world

Who knows what awaits? Gigantic monsters? Lifeless marine void? The ocean is discovered only by 0.001%.

Survive as an aquatic creature

Find and eat nutrients, or maybe other forms of aquatic life. Migrate to new regions in search of better life.

Stay in the water if you want

And when you evolve enough to leave the water, you can refuse. Stay back here, relax, and lead your species to sapience underwater.(Yes, smeltal the meltal)

Gain experience

Your creature is a separate individual, who ages and gains experience from performing certain actions and tasks. The more experience - the stronger and more valuable it is.

Find best evolutionary path

You have a lot of evolutionary traits to choose from; each is a different path, affecting the gameplay in all possible ways. What is the best one?

Absolute customisation

You can edit external and internal organs of your creature, adding as much detail as you want. Evolutionary law is your only enemy - putting three brains into your creature won't give it thrice the intelligence.

Organise your tribe

Turn your small settlement into a big neolithic city, seek and extract resources in all parts of the world, produce and trade unique goods.

Control each individual separately

Each tribe member has their own inventory, personal traits and tools. You need to take care of all of them, and they will lead you to victory.

Research technologies

Advance your technique, teach yourself new ways to produce weapons, build better buildings and create a utopian society. You only need to come through 3 substrages: Mesolithic, Paleolithic and Neolithic.

Develop unique culture

Each tribe has its own unique culture and genetics, that define its appearance and traditions. Some of them are good fishermen, some are excellent hunters, and some, maybe, love to live in the mountains.

Rule a whole civilisation

Advance through 3 substages, covering Metal Age, Middle Ages and Exploration Age; control resources, build cities, castles and villages, trade and ally with other countries, maintain your diplomatic policy.

Command gigantic armies

You now command whole armies with lots of proffesional soldiers and knights in them on the battlefield. Equip them with custom weapon and armour, develop your own strategy and win the enemy through quality or quantity.

Decide your ideology and policy

A kingdom or a republic? A country that values religion or science? A country that believes in dominance of its own culture? Traditions and policies of your people are for you to decide and develop.

Step into idustrialisation!

Go through 3 substages of industrial society: Industrial, Post-Industrial and Futuristic (Metaindustrial). You now have factories to build, a whole new social class to care about, political changes and unstoppable technological progress that can ruin your civilisation. Will your planet unite?

Lead vehicles and machines into the battle

Your armies are now using vehicles like tanks or airplanes, and your armies no longer use any armour or engage in close-melee combat.

Touch the stars

Post-Industrial society is no longer totally bound to its home planet. You can launch satellites, visit your moon (if you have it) or start colonising whole new planets. But yet, only inside your solar system, constantly racing with other countries.

A whole interstellar empire

Congratulations on uniting your planet! You now travel between the stars with whatever technology you discovered, and control every single ship, giving commands in a grand-strategy form.

Colonise and observe new planets

Exploit the resources of distant planets, find and settle habitable worlds, and each one of them can be viewed by you - every small settlement and landscape detail. See how your empire works in real time, build and shape cities.

Galactic diplomacy

Of course your species always knew you were not alone in this universe. Other empires are your rivals - or maybe you'll be able to turn them into your allies through masterful diplomacy?

A godlike ascension

No, the story doesn't end here. The science will progress eternally - into building Megastructures, travelling other galaxies, modifying your species into something completely new and starting new life. Or maybe, you would want to escape into simulation? Or give yourself extraordinary psychic powers, that connect with different dimensions?
